## Areas where AI assistance was used

1. **Experiment planning and methodology:** assistance in organizing the seven experiments, describing architectures and loss functions, planning evaluation protocols, and preparing reproducibility checks.
2. **Notebook development:** AI-generated or AI-assisted drafts of Google Colab notebook code, including model definitions, training loops, evaluation routines, checkpoint handling, and plotting. Alternative notebook implementations were compared before selecting the versions used for the experiments.
3. **Debugging and review:** assistance diagnosing notebook errors, reviewing verification checks, and suggesting corrections. The student executed the notebooks in Google Colab and reviewed the resulting runtime output.
4. **Experiment interpretation:** assistance interpreting training histories, reconstruction metrics, FID, latency, confusion-matrix results, and generated-image visualizations. Final claims must remain grounded in the saved experiment artifacts.
5. **Figures and documentation:** assistance combining selected experiment figures, organizing metric tables, and drafting repository/report documentation.

## Student responsibilities and verification

The student selected the final experiment notebooks, executed the full experiments in Google Colab on an NVIDIA Tesla T4 GPU, retained the resulting checkpoints and experiment artifacts in Google Drive, and reviewed the final verification outputs. The reported metrics are taken from the experiment JSON/CSV artifacts rather than invented or substituted values.

The student is responsible for understanding the submitted architectures, code, mathematical explanations, evaluation protocol, and conclusions, and for checking the final report and repository before submission. AI-generated content is treated as draft assistance, not as an authoritative source. Any statement unsupported by the actual experiment artifacts or cited references must be removed or qualified.

## Scope and limitations of this disclosure

This disclosure describes the AI assistance used during experiment planning, notebook development/review, debugging, interpretation, and documentation preparation. It does not imply that AI independently ran the experiments or verified results on the student's behalf. Actual model training and saved experiment outputs were produced by the student's Google Colab runs.
