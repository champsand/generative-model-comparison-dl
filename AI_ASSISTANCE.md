# AI Assistance Disclosure

## Project

**Project:** Comparative Study of Generative Models on Fashion-MNIST  
**Repository:** https://github.com/champsand/generative-model-comparison-dl

## AI tools used

- Claude (Anthropic)
- ChatGPT (OpenAI)

## How the tools were used

Claude and ChatGPT were used as supporting tools for brainstorming and coding assistance.

For the seven experiment notebooks, AI tools helped draft and discuss parts of the Python code, including model definitions, training-step code, evaluation routines, checkpoint handling, and plotting. The suggested code was reviewed and adjusted before it was used.

AI assistance was also used to organise the reusable source files under `src/`. This included model definitions, data utilities, loss functions, one-batch training helpers, evaluation functions, and supporting utilities.

## Work carried out by me

I was responsible for the project decisions and experiment work, including:

- Selecting the models and methods used in the comparison.
- Deciding the experiment setup and configurations.
- Planning the work and deciding how to carry out the experiments.
- Running the training and evaluation in Google Colab.
- Collecting and checking the metrics, training histories, and generated figures.
- Reviewing the results and preparing the interpretation and conclusions.

The training and evaluation runs reported in the repository were executed by me in Google Colab. Claude and ChatGPT did not run the experiments or produce the saved metrics.

## Review and responsibility

AI-generated or AI-suggested code was treated as a draft and reviewed before execution. I am responsible for understanding the submitted code, methods, results, and explanations, and for checking that the final report matches the saved experiment evidence.
